Market Lens is a free, open-source desktop app for researching stocks, inspired by the load-a-stock-and-go workflow of professional terminals. It runs on your computer (Tauri + Rust + SQLite), pulls data from free sources with your own keys, and paper-trades only.

## What's in 0.3.0

### One page per stock
Load a ticker once and every view follows it: a grid of live panels you can open into full views, or jump to with terminal-style codes (`AAPL HDS`, `TSLA DCF`, `NVDA SPLC`). Esc goes back to the grid; recent stocks sit in the sidebar.

- **Description (DES):** profile, key statistics, risk metrics, research journal, reported and upcoming earnings against estimates.
- **Chart (GP):** candles or line, ranges, moving averages, comparison overlay, SVG export.
- **Financials (FA):** annual and quarterly statements, SEC revenue history.
- **News (CN):** the last week of company news.
- **Holders (HDS):** every institution with a 13F position, changes vs last quarter, conviction (% of the manager's portfolio), 5%+ owners (13D/G), insiders (Forms 3/4/5), options, ownership history, CSV export.
- **Ownership changes (OWN):** one dated feed of the biggest fund moves, 5%+ owner filings and insider buys and sales.
- **Suppliers & customers (SPLC):** a relationship graph built from annual reports, with sourced evidence.
- **Valuation (DCF):** a five-year DCF pre-filled from the company's SEC filings, each input labelled with its source; bear/base/bull and sensitivity.
- **Time & sales (TAS):** live trade prints with VWAP and a buy/sell estimate; the last session's trades when the market is closed.
- **Filings (CF):** recent 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K and more.

Key statistics (P/E, margin, revenue growth, market cap, 52-week range) are calculated from the live price, trailing-four-quarter statements and SEC share counts, with each figure's source shown.

### S&P 500 market map
The Screener opens on a heatmap of every S&P 500 company: grouped by sector, sized by market cap, coloured by the change over 1 day to 1 year. Click any company to open its stock page.

### Also
- Real-time US prices (Finnhub stream), daily history (Twelve Data), statements (Alpha Vantage), past trades (Alpaca), economy and commodities (FRED), live vessel map (AISStream), SEC EDGAR filings and 13F data.
- A directory of about 267,000 listings on 90 exchanges.
- Paper trading with a full ledger, portfolio analytics, price alerts.
- Data loads by itself, is saved locally, and resumes where it left off after a restart.

## Install
- **macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon):** download the `.dmg`. The app isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time, right-click the app → **Open**, or allow it under **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** run the `.exe` installer (SmartScreen may ask you to confirm).
- **Linux:** `.deb` or `.AppImage`.

On first launch, paste the free keys you have (Twelve Data, Finnhub, Alpha Vantage, Alpaca, FRED, SEC contact email). One is enough to start; **Demo data** works with none. All trades are simulated, and nothing here is investment advice.
